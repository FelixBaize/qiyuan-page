We turn expert knowledge into finished explainer videos, using an automated production pipeline built on Claude.

What we do

Specialists such as lawyers have deep knowledge but little time to produce video. They give us a draft script; we deliver a finished, edited lecture video. Doing it by hand took 6 to 9 hours of editing per episode. Our pipeline does most of that work automatically.

How it works

  1. Script. Claude rewrites the expert's draft for spoken delivery and flags factual points for the expert to confirm.
  2. Voice and presenter. Text-to-speech narration and an AI presenter are generated from the approved script.
  3. Edit. Our own tool drives DaVinci Resolve to assemble the timeline: layouts, b-roll, captions and motion, following a written style guide.
  4. Review. Clients comment on scripts and cuts through a shared review page, and changes go back into the pipeline.

Claude Code is how we build and run this pipeline day to day, from the editing tool itself to each episode's production.
